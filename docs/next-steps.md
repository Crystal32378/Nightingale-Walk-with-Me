# Next steps: family preparation and hospital volunteers

Nightingale aims to make the final stretch to hospital care easier for wheelchair users, older adults and caregivers. The current bilingual prototype serves **one field-checked route to one hospital**. Family route preparation, route sharing and a volunteer authoring interface are **planned capabilities**, not features of the current preview.

## Prepare a journey from afar

The proposed family workflow lets an adult child, relative or friend prepare a route for an older family member before a hospital visit:

1. Choose an already locally verified route to the intended hospital entrance.
2. Review its starting point, landmarks, access notes and most recent verification date.
3. Share a simple route link that opens the walking interface at the agreed starting point.
4. If the required route is missing, request or prepare a draft for local review.

The preparation screen should be separate from the walker's interface, which stays focused on one clear next action. Remote preparation cannot verify current physical conditions. A draft needs local field checks before it can become navigable.

## Help hospitals build and maintain local routes

A proposed volunteer pilot would adapt the first route's **scout → annotate → verify** process:

| Role | Proposed responsibility |
|---|---|
| Hospital volunteer or local contributor | Walk the route; document elevators, ramps, crossings, signs, entrance evidence and likely wrong turns. |
| AI-assisted preparation tools | Organize photos and notes, propose draft landmark labels and guidance, and flag ambiguities for review. |
| Human route reviewer | Check proposals against the field evidence; correct errors; approve the route version and record its verification date. |
| Local route maintainer | Recheck changed signage, construction or access conditions; update or withdraw affected routes. |

The first route involved three author-reported field visits of about 10–15 minutes each and a 57-image human-reviewed reference set. See [route development and evidence limits](route-development.md). This is one route's experience, not an onboarding benchmark or a guarantee that three visits suffice elsewhere.

Volunteer participation and hospital partnerships are proposals; no partnership or staffing commitment is claimed. AI-generated drafts would remain subject to human approval, preserving the current principle: **AI interprets. Verified data decides.**

## Validate outcomes before wider rollout

The proposed next pilot would include wheelchair users, older adults, caregivers and relatives preparing a journey remotely. Full outdoor acceptance of the current English software remains pending.

Measures to collect include route completion, wrong turns, requests for help, and whether relatives can prepare the correct route without assistance. A volunteer pilot should also measure the full preparation effort, review corrections and maintenance workload. These are proposed measures; no outcome rates, time savings or scalability results are claimed yet.

The frozen `en-preview-2026-10-08` version remains the demo baseline. This roadmap changes documentation only; it does not introduce a route editor, publish new route data, or change the deployed application.
